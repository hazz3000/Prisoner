Each bar is one circle, sorted best to worst. Small circles are pulled toward the average so that luck doesn't make them look hot. A flat line means there's nothing to follow.

What the strategy would have gained

Before you act on this

  1. This is evidence, not proof. The checks show whether the conditions were there. Only a split test shows the gain: split your circles into two random halves, run hot circles + retry on one half and your usual targeting on the other, with the same budget for 2–4 weeks.
  2. Aim for at least 100 conversions per half, or the test can't tell a real gain from luck.
  3. IP data is personal data in the EU and UK. Make sure you have a legal basis to keep and use it, and prefer circle totals where you can.
