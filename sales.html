How it works

STEP 1Group your audience

IP addresses are grouped into small local circles. Nobody is profiled one by one; only totals per circle are counted.

STEP 2Spend where it sells

Every day, more of the budget moves to the circles bringing in sales, and their neighbours. Circles that don't buy get less.

STEP 3Rest, then retry

Someone who just saw the ad gets a break before seeing it again, so the best circles aren't worn out.

Want to know if this fits your audience? Load a past campaign and the checker will tell you whether your areas differ enough for this to pay off.

Estimates come from simulating your numbers. "Typical" assumes the best fifth of areas converts around 3× the average; "Cautious" assumes about 1.8×; "Strong" nearly 4×. Results depend on how different your areas really are. The way to know for sure is to split your areas in two and run both approaches side by side for 2–4 weeks. In the EU and UK, IP addresses count as personal data, so targeting by IP needs a legal basis.
